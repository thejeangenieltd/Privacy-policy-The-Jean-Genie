The Jean Genie Inventory — Privacy Policy

Draft prepared 10 October 2026 — review and confirm before publication

The Jean Genie Inventory is an internal tool being developed to help The Jean Genie manage its own eBay listings and coordinate stock availability across marketplaces.

Information accessed

When authorised by the account holder, the application may access eBay seller listing information, such as listing identifiers, SKUs and listing status, to check availability and end listings for items sold elsewhere. The initial version is not designed to collect buyer names, addresses or payment details.

Purpose and legal basis

Information is used solely to operate and secure the seller's authorised inventory-management workflow. Where UK data protection law applies, the intended lawful basis is legitimate interests in operating the business, subject to an appropriate assessment.

Storage and retention

The initial design is to process eBay API listing responses temporarily without retaining eBay-derived listing or account data in the inventory spreadsheet. Authentication credentials and tokens, if issued, must be stored securely by the application operator and retained only while required to operate the authorised connection. This section must be updated if the actual implementation differs.

Sharing and processors

The tool is intended for internal business use. Service providers used for hosting or automation may process technical information necessary to run the integration. This policy will be updated to identify relevant providers once selected. Information is not intended to be sold or used for advertising.

Security

Access will be limited to authorised operators. OAuth credentials and tokens must not be published in source code or public webpages. Security arrangements will be finalised before live operation.

Your rights and contact

Where personal data is processed, individuals may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, and to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office. For privacy enquiries contact: thejeangenieltd@gmail.com.

Updates

This notice will be reviewed and updated before the integration goes live and whenever its data processing changes.
